REI 7.2
Changelog.

A fresh canvas for every part of your setup. Explore the new tools, controls, and creative touches in this release.

35new featuresACROSS 04 AREAS
4 FEATURE AREAS TAP A CARD TO EXPLORE
01LOCKSCREEN / 05 UPDATESLockscreen & clocksMore control, from preview to apply.
  • Lockscreen clock picker for built-in and saved clocks
  • Wallpaper preview and drag-to-position
  • Bottom clock layouts and lock-icon visibility control
  • Instant clock switching without restarting SystemUI
  • Improved clock sizing and positioning
02CREATION / 14 UPDATESClock editorA faster, deeper canvas for your ideas.
  • Canvas-first editor with contextual tabs
  • Floating, resizable editor panel with bottom docking
  • Multi-select, batch transforms, alignment guides, snapping, and Smart Layout
  • Element rotation slider and copy-size controls
  • Named layer groups, group duplication, and My Widgets
  • Copy, paste, undo, redo, history, and save toolbar
  • AI photo cutout
  • Image fills, smart text fills, GIF autoplay, crop and zoom controls
  • Shared image fills across shapes
  • More shapes and individual corner controls
  • Gradient, transparency, and tint-opacity controls
  • Frosted glass, curved text, arc text, and Kinetic Text
  • Depth wallpaper placement, presets, parallax, and peek-behind gestures
  • More animation styles and per-clock effect settings
03DAILY LIFE / 08 UPDATESWidgets, music & weatherUseful glanceable tools, with more personality.
  • New music widgets, playback controls, and audio-reactive visuals
  • Quick Controls, hourly forecasts, and Cyber Core telemetry
  • Battery, memory, storage, uptime, alarm, user-info, and weather widgets
  • New weather icon packs and effects
  • Improved rain, water drops, steam, and condensation
  • Productivity, habit, occasion, countdown, and focus widgets
  • Quick-add tasks, expandable lists, and multiple task cards
  • Productivity templates, AOD support, haptics, and habit rollover
04STYLE / 08 UPDATESVisuals & communityMore ways to create, collect, and share.
  • Animated poster clocks and reusable card assemblies
  • More animated 3D companions: Anya, Homare, Miku, and Puff
  • Companion motion, gaze, expressions, dialogue, and touch reactions
  • Expanded font library with bulk downloads
  • Community Clock Library with search, favorites, and filters
  • Offline clock catalog and package caching
  • Clock packages retain bundled images and custom fonts
  • Selectable retro LED-matrix icons and new system card styles
